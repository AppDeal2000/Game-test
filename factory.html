HOW TO PLAY

  • Your factory has 3 workstations connected by buffers.
  • Each station processes items at a different base speed.
  • You have 12 workers to assign across all stations.

  • -5 pts for every item lost to buffer overflow.
  • Keep buffers from filling up — red = danger!
  • Find the optimal balance to maximize throughput. You have 90 seconds.
TIP: Downstream stations are slower — they need more workers!
